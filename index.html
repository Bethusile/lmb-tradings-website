ABOUT LMB TRADINGS • HOMEMAKERS CO.

LMB Tradings (Pty) Ltd t/a LMB Homemakers Co. is a private, 100% black-owned South African building contractor, property development partner, and project management group servicing the Mthatha-East London corridor in the Eastern Cape along with the Western Cape, and Gauteng.

We operate with a mission to deliver superior residential construction, turnkey property investments, structural renovations, and rigorous project management. Backed by highly skilled tradesmen, certified artisans, and specialized subcontractors, we ensure every shelter is built to enduring engineering precision.

"Driving radical transformation in property development & investing across the Eastern Cape province, reinforcing 'Support Local' in South African property development."
