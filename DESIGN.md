## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an executive, high-trust industrial aesthetic tailored for large-scale construction, civil engineering, and infrastructure development. Drawing inspiration from heavyweight corporate engineering leaders like WBHO, the design narrative balances raw construction power with boardroom credibility, governance, and institutional reliability.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Commercial property developers, institutional investors, government infrastructure bodies, industrial project managers, and high-value residential/commercial clients.
- **Emotional Response:** Solidity, operational rigor, uncompromising safety, and architectural longevity. The experience projects a team capable of moving earth, pouring foundations, and delivering multi-million-dollar developments on time and within spec.

### Design Movement: Industrial Precision / High-Contrast Corporate
The system merges **Corporate Modernism** with **Industrial Structural Realism**:
- **Geometry & Architecture:** Monolithic vertical grid alignments, structured card bands, and precise linear division planes reminiscent of blueprints and steel frameworks.
- **Visual Tension:** Heavy industrial safety yellow accents against deep naval bedrock (`#0B1325`) and crisp drafting whites.
- **Tactility:** Crisp structural borders, high-contrast metric counters, and authoritative typographic hierarchies that communicate monumental scale without unnecessary decorative fluff.

## Colors

The color architecture is derived from heavy civil engineering equipment, high-visibility safety markers, structural steel, and deep executive corporate navy.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#F5C400`):** LMB Signature Industrial Yellow. Used for critical conversion triggers, primary interaction anchors, high-visibility category badges, and active navigational indicators. Never used for long-form text.
- **Secondary (`#0B1325`):** Deep Bedrock Slate Navy. Serves as the executive foundation—utilized in mastheads, global navigation bars, monumental metric callouts, and dark structural hero panels.
- **Tertiary (`#E5B200`):** Darker Ochre/Caterpillar Yellow. Applied to hover states, active borders, and visual accents against light backdrops to preserve accessible contrast.
- **Neutral (`#131E35`):** Structural Slate. Used for high-contrast dark surfaces, secondary headers, and heavy typography on light backgrounds.

### Light & Dark Section Harmony
While the default color mode is `light` (crisp architectural white `#FFFFFF` and concrete light `#F4F5F7`), primary sections alternate rhythmically into deep naval slate blocks (`#0B1325`) to anchor flagship civil project showcases, engineering stats, and executive governance disclosures.

### Accessibility Rules
- Text placed over `#F5C400` must strictly use `#0B1325` or `#111418`. Pure white text over yellow is strictly prohibited.
- For light-themed body copy, `#131E35` and `#4A5568` provide minimum 7:1 contrast ratios on `#FFFFFF` and `#F4F5F7`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy communicates industrial magnitude and architectural precision.

### Type Pairings & Construction Rationale
- **Display & Headlines (Barlow Condensed):** Evoking technical blueprint schematics, highway signage, and heavy equipment labeling. The condensed verticality allows prominent, large-scale uppercase impact for titles without taking over horizontal real estate.
- **Body & Editorial (Source Sans 3):** Clean, structural, highly legible humanist sans-serif designed for corporate clarity, technical documentation, tender contract summaries, and project specifications.
- **Data & Microcopy (Barlow Condensed in All Caps):** Used for engineering labels, project category tags, tracking numbers, and metric counters, creating an authoritative, standardized system.

### Text Styling Directives
- Primary sectional headlines and hero claims are rendered in **FULL UPPERCASE** with a subtle letter-spacing adjustment (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to mirror executive industrial standards.
- Body text retains natural sentence case with comfortable 1.5–1.6 line height ratios to ensure long reports and tender briefs remain effortlessly readable.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column structural grid system that prioritizes masonry balance, structural stability, and rhythmic discipline.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1280px - 1440px):** 12-column grid, centered with `margin-lg` (64px) outer gutters and `gutter-lg` (32px) between columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid with `margin-md` (40px) outer padding and `gutter` (24px) column gaps.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin` (16px) margins and `space-md` (16px) column gaps.

### Architectural Alignment Directives
- **Structural Card Arrays:** Project portfolio items sit in rigid 4-column (triad) or 3-column (quad) modular configurations with 0px or tight 1px engineering border dividers.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major thematic transitions (such as Corporate Profile into Active Tenders) employ `space-3xl` (96px) padding to command respect and executive clarity.
- **Stat Panels:** Prominent multi-column counter ribbons snap across the full viewport width with uniform vertical divider borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects soft, floating or blurry consumer-app shadows in favor of **Structural Rigor, Crisp Engineering Borders, and Tonal Stacking**.

### Architectural Elevation Planes
1. **Base Foundation (Ground Level 0):** Architectural pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale concrete tint (`#F4F5F7`). Cards and elements rest flat without dropped drop-shadows, framed cleanly with 1px `#D8DEE9` technical borders.
2. **Structural Tonal Layers (Level 1):** Sub-panels, statistics containers, and data tables shift color values into bedrock navy (`#0B1325`) or muted navy (`#131E35`). Depth is created through massive visual contrast rather than blur.
3. **Interactive Lift (Level 2):** When interactive components like project cards or service panels are hovered, they introduce a distinct 4px solid industrial yellow bottom accent bar (`#F5C400`) and an intentional, low-spread ambient lift: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(11, 19, 37, 0.18)`.
4. **Hero Overlays & Modal Modifiers (Level 3):** Modal overlays, technical plan viewers, and mobile navigation sheets utilize deep slate backdrop tints (`#0B1325` at 85% opacity) with zero blur, reinforcing solid, impenetrable structure.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is purely **monolithic and geometric (0px corner radius)**. 

### Geometric Principles
- **Right Angles Only:** All buttons, cards, image containers, input fields, and category tags possess crisp `0px` corners. This reflects structural steel, reinforced precast concrete blocks, and drafting blueprints.
- **Industrial Accents:** Diagonal 45-degree corner notches or chamfers may be applied via CSS clip-paths exclusively on special badge tags or industrial stat banners to evoke heavy industrial machinery plates.
- **Iconography Framing:** Icons and technical indicators are either housed inside unrounded square containers or hexagonal wireframe containers matching the corporate mark geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** High-vis industrial yellow background (`#F5C400`), solid 0px border radius, sharp uppercase text (`label-caps`) in `#0B1325`. Padding: `16px 32px`. On hover: transitions to `#E5B200` with an animated rightward arrow icon displacement (+4px).
- **Executive Navy Button:** Deep slate-navy background (`#0B1325`), white uppercase text, 1px solid `#0B1325`. On hover: yellow text accent and 2px bottom border in `#F5C400`.
- **Engineering Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 2px solid `#0B1325` (or `#FFFFFF` on dark backgrounds), uppercase typography with `0.1em` tracking. Hover fills the button with the outline color.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Project Capability Cards:** Zero border radius. 100% width imagery with high-contrast aspect ratios (16:10 or 4:3). Text sits directly beneath on a crisp concrete-grey (`#F4F5F7`) plate or overlays the image with a heavy slate-to-transparent linear gradient (`#0B1325` at base). 
- **Top Accent Line:** Featured projects showcase a persistent 3px top border in `#F5C400`.

### Data Metrics & Stat Counters
- Monolithic numeric values rendered in `stat-metric` (`Barlow Condensed`, 64px bold, uppercase).
- Directly beneath the number sits a 2px horizontal yellow line (`width: 32px; height: 2px; background: #F5C400`), followed by a 2-line condensed label in full uppercase tracking.

### Forms & Input Fields
- **Text Inputs & Selectors:** Sharp 0px corners, 48px height, 1px solid border (`#D8DEE9`). Background is `#FFFFFF`. Text set in `Source Sans 3` at 16px.
- **Focus State:** 2px solid border in `#0B1325` with an accompanying left border accent in `#F5C400`. No rounded glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Crisp 18px × 18px squares with sharp 0px corners and a 2px solid `#0B1325` frame. When checked, filled with `#0B1325` and an industrial yellow check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Pure concentric circles (18px) with a heavy navy ring and a solid `#F5C400` center node on active state.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict rectangular tags (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Background `#131E35`, font color `#FFFFFF`, or yellow highlight background (`#FFF3B3`) with `#0B1325` text. Font: `label-caps` at 12px with 0.1em tracking.

### Navigation Header & Industrial Footer
- **Header:** Rigid horizontal bar with a deep navy ground (`#0B1325`) or pure white with a 2px bottom yellow divider rule. Navigation links are rendered in `Barlow Condensed` uppercase with yellow underline accents on hover/active states.
- **Footer:** Imposing dark slate anchor (`#0B1325`) featuring multi-column sitemaps, statutory compliance badges, tender contact routes, and clear headquarters registration details.
