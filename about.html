ABOUT THE COMPANY

Who Builds With LMB Tradings

LMB Tradings (Pty) Ltd, trading as LMB Homemakers Co., is a private, 100% black-owned, B-BBEE Level 1 building contractor servicing the Mthatha-East London corridor, Eastern Cape. Here's the leadership behind the company, the full range of what we build, and our official registration details.

PROFILE OF MEMBERS

Leadership

engineering

Lwenzo Mafumana

Director & Founder · +9 years experience

A property developer and investor born and bred in Mthatha, Eastern Cape. Lwenzo grew up working alongside his father in the construction environment (City Builders CC), which led him to pursue a career in engineering for the administrative and technical grounding to run his own business.

He holds a National Diploma in Industrial Engineering and an Advanced Diploma in Business Management, and went on to work as a Projects Engineer for Lear Corporation E-Systems & Logistics and The SPAR Group Ltd. He founded LMB Tradings after also acquiring certificates in Project Management, Construction Management, Corporate Finance and Architectural Drawings, and holds an NQF Level 2 qualification in Wood-Machining & Cabinetmaking.

Lwenzo guarantees every client and project his personal attention, from first quote to final handover.
